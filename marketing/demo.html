Control Center

PeakLogic staff

PeakLogic's own command plane — the updated & revised Super Admin Console (the Platform Control Center): fleet Overview by fan-out (never a cross-tenant query), audited act-as into any tenant or partner, device twins, firmware, and the unified-platform Operations views (PeakView360, Hubs, CMMS, Compliance, PeakAssist).

Who logs in: PeakLogic operators. Never a customer or partner.

Operator app PeakView360 — the live HMI/SCADA view (process screens, alarms, historian trends, equipment PdM, interactive Facility View). Launched from within any portal for a given site — served by the on-site Hub over the LAN, offline-capable. It is not a separate login surface. Preview it standalone ↗ :5175
The hierarchy, plainly: a customer of Ace logs into the Customer Portal (their sites only) — not Ace's partner portal. Ace uses the Partner Portal (all their customers). PeakLogic staff run the Control Center. And PeakView360 opens inside any of these for a live site view. Three portals, one operator app, one core.
Edge PeakLogic Hubs — on-site gateways that acquire from PLCs/RTUs, serve PeakView360 + PeakAssist on the LAN, and store-and-forward telemetry. PeakAssist provides contextual help across every surface.
Core PeakLogicSystems — the multi-tenant Azure cloud: row-level-security data plane, IoT ingestion, AI analytics, CMMS, compliance automation, and an immutable audit log. Every surface above is a view onto this one core, scoped to who's looking.
