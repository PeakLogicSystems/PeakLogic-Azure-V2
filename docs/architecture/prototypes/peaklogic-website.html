FAQ

Common questions

What counts as a “site”?

A physical location you monitor, such as one restaurant, one pumping station, or one building. Devices and users at that site are unlimited.

Do I have to replace my current CMMS or software?

No. PeakLogic sits on top of your existing operation and sends work into the systems you already use. It's additive, not a rip-and-replace.

Can I brand it for my customers?

Yes. On the partner plans, PeakLogic runs on your own domain with your logo and colors, so your customers see your brand.

What devices do you support?

Common field equipment across pumps, HVAC, refrigeration, energy, flow, and leak or level sensing, plus edge gateways and select third-party OEM clouds. New device types are added as a configuration.

Is my data secure and traceable?

Yes. Your data is encrypted, scoped to your organization, and every access is recorded in an append-only audit log, so activity is fully traceable.
