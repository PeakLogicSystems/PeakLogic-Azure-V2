FAQ

Common questions

What counts as a “site”?

A physical location you monitor — one restaurant, one pumping station, one building. Devices and users at that site are unlimited.

Do I have to replace my current CMMS or software?

No. PeakLogic overlays your existing operation and pushes work into the systems you already use. It's additive, not a rip-and-replace.

How does outcome-based pricing work?

For qualifying Enterprise programs, part of your fee is tied to the measured downtime, energy, and insurance claims we help you avoid — so you pay on value delivered.

What about hardware?

Devices can be purchased outright, leased, or subsidized through our commercial insurance partners as part of a risk-mitigation program.

Is my data secure and isolated?

Yes. Every organization's data is isolated at the database level (row-level security), encrypted, and fully audited. SOC 2 is in progress.
