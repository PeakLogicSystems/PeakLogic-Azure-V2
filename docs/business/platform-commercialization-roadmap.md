# Platform Commercialization Roadmap

**Status:** Draft v0.1
**Prepared by:** PeakLogic
**Parties:** PeakLogic and Purple Standard — under evaluation for a potential merger and a shared go-to-market
**Scope:** Vendor-neutral joint planning framework. This document does not presume which company's platform, brand, or technology stack serves as the technical foundation going forward — that determination is outside the scope of this roadmap and depends on the outcome of merger discussions.
**Last updated:** 2026-07-17

---

## 0. Purpose & Context

PeakLogic and Purple Standard are each developing platforms in overlapping IoT-enabled facilities/risk-management space, and are evaluating a merger paired with a common commercialization roadmap. This document lays out a phased plan to take **"the platform"** — whichever combination of the two organizations' technology, product, and go-to-market assets the merger produces — from its current state to a scalable, revenue-generating, multi-vertical commercial product.

Where this document says "the platform," it refers to the eventual combined/agreed technical and commercial foundation, not a specific existing codebase. Where it says "the companies" or "both parties," it refers jointly to PeakLogic and Purple Standard. Decisions about whose existing architecture, product name, or brand carries forward are explicitly deferred to the merger negotiation and are not assumed by this roadmap.

---

## 1. Objective

Transform the combined organizations' existing platform work into a scalable, revenue-generating, multi-vertical commercial product.

---

## 2. Phase 1 — Platform Assessment & Modernization (Days 0–90)

**Goal:** Establish a stable, scalable foundation capable of supporting commercial growth.

### Key Actions
- Conduct a full technical audit of both companies' existing platform architectures
- Identify gaps in reliability, scalability, and data ingestion across both platforms
- Define unified platform standards covering asset intelligence, risk scoring, telemetry, and service automation
- Prioritize a joint modernization backlog
- Establish core KPIs (ARR, adoption, retention, ticket generation, uptime)

### Deliverables
- Platform modernization plan
- Updated architecture blueprint
- 12-month technical roadmap
- KPI dashboard framework

---

## 3. Phase 2 — Productization & Packaging (Days 90–180)

**Goal:** Convert the platform from an internal/dual-company tool into a single market-ready product.

### Key Actions
- Define product tiers (Basic, Pro, Enterprise)
- Create pricing models (per-location, per-asset, per-service-event)
- Build onboarding workflows and customer-facing UI improvements
- Develop documentation, SLAs, and support structure
- Establish an integration strategy (POS, sensors, IoT gateways, service systems)

### Deliverables
- Commercial product packages
- Pricing & billing model
- Customer onboarding experience
- Integration roadmap

---

## 4. Phase 3 — Market Validation & Pilot Deployment (Days 180–270)

**Goal:** Prove commercial viability with real customers and refine product-market fit.

### Key Actions
- Select 3–5 pilot customers across target verticals
- Deploy the platform with full monitoring and risk scoring
- Measure ticket-generation uplift and operational impact
- Gather customer feedback and refine features
- Validate pricing and willingness to pay

### Deliverables
- Pilot results report
- Product-market fit validation
- Refined pricing and feature set
- Case studies demonstrating ROI

---

## 5. Phase 4 — Commercial Launch (Days 270–360)

**Goal:** Launch the platform commercially with scalable operations and revenue targets.

### Key Actions
- Build sales enablement materials
- Train internal teams (sales, ops, service)
- Launch marketing assets (website, demos, ROI calculators)
- Establish customer success and support workflows
- Set ARR targets and expansion goals

### Deliverables
- Full commercial launch
- Sales playbook
- Marketing collateral
- ARR growth plan

---

## 6. Phase 5 — Scale & Vertical Expansion (Year 2+)

**Goal:** Expand the platform into additional verticals and increase ARR through new capabilities.

### Key Actions
- Add advanced analytics (predictive risk scoring, anomaly detection)
- Introduce a hardware ecosystem (sensors, gateways, energy modules)
- Expand into new verticals (QSR, retail, hospitality, healthcare)
- Build partner integrations and reseller channels
- Optimize the platform for enterprise-scale deployments

### Deliverables
- Multi-vertical product strategy
- Hardware + software ecosystem roadmap
- Partner channel program
- ARR expansion plan (>$1M → $5M → $10M+)

---

## 7. Strategic Outcomes

- A unified, modernized platform that drives measurable service outcomes
- A commercially viable SaaS/managed-service product
- A scalable revenue engine with multi-vertical potential
- A long-term data and analytics foundation for continued growth

---

## 8. Open Items

Explicitly not addressed by this document — flagged rather than assumed:

- Which company's existing technology stack, brand, and product name carry forward (or whether both are consolidated into something new)
- Legal/corporate structure of the merger itself (entity structure, equity split, governance)
- How existing customers/contracts of each company transition during Phases 1–3
- Data migration and platform-consolidation mechanics, once a technical direction is chosen in Phase 1

**How to apply:** This roadmap should be revisited and made specific (technology choice, branding, org structure) once merger terms are further along. Until then, treat it as the shared planning skeleton both parties can execute against without prejudging outcomes still under negotiation.
