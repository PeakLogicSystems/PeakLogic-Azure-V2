Azure vs. DigitalOcean

Choosing the Right Cloud Foundation for Essential Field Services

PeakLogic delivers intelligence, control, compliance automation, and operator-assistive workflows across distributed wastewater, environmental, and facility systems. The cloud foundation behind PeakLogic must support secure IoT ingestion, multi-tenant isolation, regulatory compliance, and SCADA-adjacent reliability. This brief summarizes why Microsoft Azure is the correct strategic platform compared to DigitalOcean.

Why Azure is the right direction for PeakLogic

1

Purpose-built for IoT at scale

Azure IoT Hub, DPS, and Device Update provide secure ingestion, provisioning, and lifecycle management for thousands of distributed devices. DigitalOcean has no IoT-native services.

2

Enterprise security & compliance

Entra ID, private networking, RBAC, and compliance certifications (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO, FedRAMP) support regulated wastewater and municipal environments. DigitalOcean lacks enterprise identity and compliance depth.

3

Multi-tenant architecture for operators & municipalities

Azure PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security enables strict tenant isolation across operators, service providers, and municipalities. DigitalOcean requires custom engineering to approximate this.

4

SCADA-adjacent reliability

Azure provides global redundancy, SLAs, and event-driven pipelines required for critical operations. DigitalOcean is not designed for mission-critical industrial workloads.

5

Seamless integration with PeakLogic’s architecture

PeakLogic’s cloud-native design (IoT Hub → Functions → PostgreSQL → PeakView360) aligns directly with Azure’s strengths. DigitalOcean cannot support the ingestion, security, or compliance requirements of the platform.

6

Long-term scalability

Azure supports thousands of sites, millions of messages, and enterprise integrations. DigitalOcean is optimized for small apps and early-stage projects.

Executive summary

Azure is a full-scale enterprise cloud platform built for IoT, security, compliance, and global scale. DigitalOcean is a developer-friendly cloud built for simple applications.

For a platform delivering intelligence, control, compliance, and risk reduction across essential field services, Azure provides the reliability, security, and operational depth PeakLogic requires.
